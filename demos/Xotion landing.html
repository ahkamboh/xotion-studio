Questions

You're wondering.

How fast is a render? +

A 15-second 1080p video takes about 45–60 seconds on the Pro queue. Studio plans get a 4× faster priority queue (around 12–15s per render). Free tier renders queue behind paid traffic and can take 2–5 minutes at busy hours.

What formats do I get? +

MP4 (H.264, posts everywhere), WebM (best for the web), and a GIF preview for Slack & email. 4K is a Studio-tier add-on. Aspect ratios: 9:16, 1:1, 4:5, and 16:9. You can render any composition at any aspect.

Can I bring my own brand fonts & colors? +

Yes — Pro and Studio both let you upload a brand kit (logo, hex palette, custom .woff2 fonts) and templates inherit it automatically. Free tier locks you to our six built-in palettes.

Is the music actually licensed? +

All soundtracks are either procedurally generated (we own them; you get a perpetual commercial license) or licensed from our partners for unlimited social use. Nothing in Xotion will get your post flagged.

Can I edit the output in Premiere / CapCut? +

The MP4 is a standard H.264 file — drop it into any editor. We also export a HyperFrames source folder (HTML + assets) on Studio so you can fork the composition entirely.

Do you watermark Pro / Studio renders? +

No. Pro and Studio renders are clean. Free renders get a small "Made with Xotion" mark in the bottom corner.

Refunds? +

30 days, no questions. Reply to any onboarding email or hit the button in your dashboard. Pro-rated for unused months on annual plans.
