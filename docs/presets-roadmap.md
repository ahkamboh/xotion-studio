# Preset Roadmap — 74 Templates Across 3 Waves

This is the editorial roadmap for xotion-studio's next preset library: 74 deterministic, CSV-batchable HTML+GSAP video templates selected from a 156-candidate research pool. The pool came from 11 researchers — 8 domain sweeps of high-demand patterns (marketplace best-sellers, UI canon, social staples, AI-era motion) plus 3 invention angles (camera narratives, physical metaphors, never-mixed mechanic fusions) — then deduped, cut for engine-constraint violations and overlap with already-built presets, and ranked by demand × distinctiveness × ease. Wave 1 (top 20) ships first as the proven money-makers; wave 2 (22) deepens each category; wave 3 (32) holds the long tail and the most ambitious never-seen showpieces.

Notable merges from the dedupe pass: four star-rating candidates → `review-stars-testimonial`; comment-spotlight → `social-post-flyin`; story-poll-sticker → `poll-reveal`; before-after-wipe → `before-after-slider`; price-drop-promo → `price-slash-stinger`; pull-quote-attribution → `quote-card-reveal`; flip-clock-countdown → `split-flap-board`; model-benchmark-race → `bar-chart-race`; split-screen-versus → `stat-battle-card`; callout-line-annotation → `feature-callout-tour`; code-diff-reveal → `ai-diff-accept`; deploy-pipeline-flow → `workflow-node-build`; receipt-dolly → `receipt-printer`. Cuts removed decorative one-liners (swipe-up chevrons, FAB dials, card-tilt hovers), near-duplicates of existing presets (voice-wave-transcript vs music visualizer + reactive captions; variable-axis-wave vs TextFX pressure; changelog-cascade vs checklist; emoji-reaction-storm vs particle overlays), and the lone hard-feasibility item (zoom-thru-stack).

## Never-mixed highlights

The ten most original fusions in the roadmap — concepts with no marketplace equivalent because each welds two proven vocabularies that have never shipped together as a parametric template:

- `agent-tool-trace` — The agent-inspector UI everyone screen-records, rebuilt as designed motion: no template product anywhere renders tool-call logs as choreographed graphics.
- `mcp-plug-handshake` — Integration announcements meet physical plug-and-socket choreography; MCP is too new for marketplaces to have caught up, leaving the category empty.
- `odometer-dive` — Thousands of counter templates exist, but all filmed from outside; flying the camera between the digit drums turns a commodity into a signature shot.
- `chart-rollercoaster` — Chart reveals and ride-cams are both proven genres, yet nobody has put the camera on the pen tip of a drawing line chart.
- `toggle-ripple-mosaic` — UI switches as mosaic pixels: a micro-interaction scaled into a logo-reveal medium, unsold anywhere because it only makes sense in a DOM-native engine.
- `slider-drives-world` — One UI control driving an entire scene's hue, light, type weight, and counters simultaneously — a demo-reel cliché in concept, absent as a batchable template.
- `take-a-number` — Deli-ticket hardware fused with waitlist-launch culture; per-signup personalization makes each render shareable, which static waitlist graphics can't do.
- `pixel-pullback` — Starts inside one named customer's tile and pulls back to the brand mosaic; per-recipient personalization of the starting cell is impossible in stock templates.
- `fine-print-loupe` — Real optical magnification (scaled clone in a clipped lens) plus a camera dive through the glass — a TV-news visual no template marketplace has parameterized.
- `counter-shatter` — The counter doesn't just hit the milestone, it physically breaks the screen; shatter transitions and odometers both exist, but never as cause and effect.
